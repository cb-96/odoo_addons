# Competition Engine Product Roadmap

Owner: Federation Competition Operations
Last updated: 2026-09-03
Review cadence: Every release

## Format generation completeness

Status: **Implemented for deterministic formats**

The engine supports single and double leagues, seeded knockout cups, placement brackets, match series, pools followed by knockout, championship/relegation splits, and custom acyclic multi-stage graphs. Feasibility checks expose fixture and round estimates and reject invalid configurations before generation.

Swiss pairing, ladders, and double elimination remain deliberate exclusions until their dynamic pairing, challenge, loser-bracket, and reset-final semantics can be represented completely.

## Club representative workspace

Status: **Implemented**

The task-first portal exposes deadlines, blockers, responsible party, next action, and direct source navigation for registration, roster readiness, referee duties, and result follow-up. Existing mobile and accessibility qualification remains authoritative.
