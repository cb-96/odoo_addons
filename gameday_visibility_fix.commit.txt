fix(portal): show upcoming game days for teams whose sheets have no roster yet

The team preparation page said "No upcoming game days are scheduled yet" for
teams that have published matches, and the game-day list behind its
"Review requirement" link was empty.

Cause: match sheets are created together with their matches, normally before
the team has a roster, so they carry no roster. Three places only looked at
sheets already linked to a roster:
- the "Game-day line-ups" check on /my/preparation matched
  roster_id = <the team's roster>; teams without a roster could never have
  game days, and a roster created later never saw its sheets;
- /my/match-sheets with a team or registration selected filtered on
  roster_id.season_id, which drops every unlinked sheet.

Changes
- Game days of a team come from its matches' season (division, else edition),
  not from the roster link: federation.portal.gameday.season_domain and
  team_sheets. The preparation check, the game-day list and the roster page
  all use them.
- The line-up check now says how many game days still need a line-up, and
  for a team without a roster that the roster has to be created first.
- A roster that is created now links itself to the team's draft sheets of the
  same season (and competition) that have no roster; a sheet that already has
  a roster keeps it. Existing unlinked sheets keep working: the list shows the
  roster the line-up will start from and the line-up page preselects it.
- The game-day list and the line-up page offer "Create roster" for a team
  without a roster, straight to that team's roster creation.
- _journey_list_scope accepts a season_domain callable for models whose season
  is not a plain field.
- Refresh docs/portal_sudo_inventory.json (also records the existing
  _is_club_representative lookup).
- Tests: sheets are game days without a roster link, season domain, roster
  creation links open sheets and keeps existing links, and an HTTP test for the
  preparation page and game-day list of a team without a roster.
